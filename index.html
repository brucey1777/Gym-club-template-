About Us 🏅

FIND YOUR INNER CHAMPION

Founded in 2010 and coming to York in 2026, join the fun at York's newest gymnastics club! From wobbly first cartwheels to performances and competition champions, everyone belongs here. 💛

At Champion Gymnastics Club, we are passionate about helping every child build confidence, develop new skills, and discover a love for gymnastics in a fun, supportive environment.

The club is led by Christina Fossheim, an experienced gymnast and coach who was a four-time British Aerobic Gymnastics Champion and proudly represented Great Britain at both World and European Championships. With over 20 years of coaching experience, Christina has worked with children of all abilities, from beginners enjoying recreational classes to athletes competing at international level.

Champion Gymnastics Club is dedicated to nurturing each child's individual journey. Through energetic warm-ups, creative circuits, fitness and strength games, and carefully structured skill progressions, we create an engaging atmosphere where every gymnast can thrive. Our goal is to help children grow not only in their technical ability but also in their confidence, resilience, and enjoyment of sport.
