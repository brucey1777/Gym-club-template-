💳 Payments

Term fees are paid online via secure Square payment. You'll receive a payment link by email after registering — your child's place is not confirmed until payment is received.

Wednesday lunchtime and after school clubs are paid as one payment covering the full Autumn term.
Friday Gymnastics & Acro is paid separately for each half-term block (Term 1 and Term 2).

Please pay as soon as you can to secure your child's place — spaces are limited and allocated on a first-paid basis.
